Our History

M18 Food Limited was established in 2010 with the vision to create a wider market for world foods from around the globe in UK and Europe. Since the establishment we've served the thousands of nation's retailers, restaurants, wholesalers and food businesses with products from around the globe. From humble beginnings of one small store offering a handful of foods, we now offer more than 20000 products from over 1500 beloved brands.
